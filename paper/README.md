# Hephaestus JORS metapaper — submission of record

JORS (Journal of Open Research Software) metapaper covering the Hephaestus
MQMQA engine and the open slag database family in this repository as one open-science
contribution.

- **Authors:** Michael E. Bustamante, Gabriel Bustamante (Odinzen LLC)
- **Target venue:** Journal of Open Research Software (JORS)
- **Package built:** 2026-09-09 (source: `Desktop/Bustamante_Hephaestus_JORS_submission_2026-09-09.zip`)

- `Bustamante_Hephaestus_JORS_manuscript.{md,docx,pdf}` — manuscript (md is the source, docx/pdf the reading copies)
- `Cover_Letter.docx`
- `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.{md,docx}`
- `Supplementary_S1_primer.{md,docx,pdf}` — S1 primer for non-coders

Odinzen equilibrium kernel and its method are not part of this work.
