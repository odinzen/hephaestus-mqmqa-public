Phase diagram builder · build & tune your ownyour data

Enter a two-component system's own data — each component's melting point and heat of fusion, plus the liquid interaction energy W — and the engine computes its phase diagram live from regular-solution thermodynamics. Change any value (the sliders are quickest) and watch the liquidus and eutectic move. Load a preset to start, or clear it and enter a brand-new system.

W < 0 (components attract): a deeper eutectic — the deep-eutectic-solvent regime; strongly negative → compound formation. W > 0 (components repel): a shallower eutectic that rises toward liquid immiscibility (a monotectic) as W grows.
