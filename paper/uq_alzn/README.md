# Al-Zn ESPEI uncertainty analysis (Figure 8)

Reproduces the parameter-uncertainty band in the manuscript. The MCMC refits the
liquid and fcc excess parameters to real, cited data and propagates the posterior
through the engine.

Datasets (`datasets/`), phase models, and the fit/plot scripts are here. Two things are
**not** committed and must be obtained separately:

- `alzn_espei.tdb` — the fittable database, derived from `alzn_mey.tdb` (S. an Mey,
  Z. Metallkd. 84 (1993) 451; the pycalphad-distributed copy carries a NIMS 2009
  copyright). Get `alzn_mey.tdb` from pycalphad's examples and re-expose the LIQUID and
  FCC_A1 excess coefficients as `VV0001`/`VV0002`/`VV0003` per `make_uq_band*.py`.
- `output/` — the MCMC trace and figure, regenerated by `run_fit.py` then
  `make_uq_band_full.py`.

Data sources: liquid enthalpy of mixing digitised from Fig. 12 of Dinsdale, Zobac,
Kroupa & Khvan, Calphad 68 (2020) 101723 (Debski et al.); phase boundaries from
Massalski, Binary Alloy Phase Diagrams, 2nd ed. (1990).
